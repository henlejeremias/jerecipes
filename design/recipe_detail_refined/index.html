Spicy Thai Red Curry

A luxurious, aromatic infusion of kaffir lime leaves, lemongrass, and rich coconut milk. This authentic blend balances fiery spice with creamy textures for a true atelier dining experience.

Ingredients

  • Coconut Milk Full-fat, organic preferred
    400ml
  • Red Curry Paste Authentic Thai brand
    3 tbsp
  • Chicken Breast Thinly sliced strips
    500g
  • Bamboo Shoots Drained and rinsed
    1 cup
  • Thai Basil & Kaffir Lime Fresh leaves
    Handful

Chef's Secret

"Fry the curry paste in a small amount of coconut cream first until the oil separates. This releases the aromatic oils for a deeper flavor profile."

Instructions

01

Bloom the Paste

In a heavy-bottomed wok or large saucepan over medium heat, add 1/2 cup of the coconut milk. Stir constantly for 2-3 minutes until it starts to bubble and thicken. Add the red curry paste and stir well to dissolve, cooking until the red oil begins to separate from the cream.

02

Sear the Protein

Add the sliced chicken breast to the wok. Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until the chicken is opaque on the outside and thoroughly coated in the fragrant paste. Ensure the heat isn't too high to avoid burning the paste.

03

Simmer and Infuse

Pour in the remaining coconut milk and add the bamboo shoots. Bring to a gentle simmer. Tear the kaffir lime leaves by hand to release their aroma and drop them into the liquid. Let the curry simmer gently for 15 minutes to allow flavors to meld.

04

Season and Garnish

Taste the sauce. Add a splash of fish sauce and a pinch of palm sugar to balance the heat. Turn off the heat and stir in the fresh Thai basil leaves. Serve immediately over jasmine rice.
